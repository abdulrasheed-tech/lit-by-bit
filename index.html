About

A team from a small city, building for everyone.

We started Lit By Bit with one belief: good software doesn’t have to come from a big city. Our team is based in Bhakkar, Pakistan, and we’re here to prove that talent from a smaller place can compete on the front line — and to create opportunities for other developers here to do the same.

We work in a small team, talk directly to the people we build for, and keep our products simple, fast and honest about what they do.

What we care about

Products that solve a real problem. Software people can trust with their files and their time. Work we can show, not just describe.
